# Export to Rodin

Send your Obsidian vault to [Rodin](https://rodin.fyi) to generate your **intellectual fingerprint** — a structured analysis of how you think, based on your notes.

## How it works

1. Install this plugin
2. Click the fingerprint icon in the ribbon (or run "Export vault to Rodin" from the command palette)
3. The plugin reads your markdown files and sends them to Rodin
4. Rodin opens in your browser with your intellectual fingerprint

## What Rodin extracts

- **Recurring themes** — your intellectual obsessions
- **Open questions** — what you're wrestling with
- **Mental models** — how you frame problems
- **Intellectual DNA** — who shaped your thinking
- **Blind spots** — what your writing avoids
- **Core question** — the single deepest question driving your thinking

## Privacy

Your vault text is sent to rodin.fyi for processing. It is not stored permanently — only the derived fingerprint is kept. See [Rodin's privacy policy](https://rodin.fyi/privacy) for details.
